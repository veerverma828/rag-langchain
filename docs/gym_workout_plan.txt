Gym Workout Plan - 4 Day Split

This is a beginner-to-intermediate 4 day push/pull/legs style workout plan focused on strength and muscle growth.

Day 1 - Push (Chest, Shoulders, Triceps)
Bench Press: 4 sets of 6-8 reps
Overhead Shoulder Press: 3 sets of 8-10 reps
Incline Dumbbell Press: 3 sets of 10 reps
Lateral Raises: 3 sets of 12-15 reps
Tricep Pushdowns: 3 sets of 12 reps

Day 2 - Pull (Back, Biceps)
Deadlifts: 3 sets of 5 reps
Pull-ups or Lat Pulldowns: 4 sets of 8-10 reps
Barbell Rows: 3 sets of 8 reps
Face Pulls: 3 sets of 15 reps
Bicep Curls: 3 sets of 12 reps

Day 3 - Rest or light cardio

Day 4 - Legs
Squats: 4 sets of 6-8 reps
Romanian Deadlifts: 3 sets of 10 reps
Leg Press: 3 sets of 10-12 reps
Calf Raises: 4 sets of 15 reps

Nutrition tip: aim for roughly 1.6 to 2.2 grams of protein per kilogram of bodyweight per day to support muscle recovery and growth. Progressive overload - gradually increasing weight or reps over time - is the key driver of long-term strength gains.

Rest 2-3 minutes between heavy compound lifts like squats and deadlifts, and 60-90 seconds between isolation exercises like curls and lateral raises.
